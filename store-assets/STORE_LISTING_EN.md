# FK Wallet – Google Play store listing (English)

## App name (max 30 characters)

FK Wallet: Card & Debt Tracker

## Short description (max 80 characters)

Track credit card debt, due dates and your monthly budget in one place.

## Full description (max 4000 characters)

FK Wallet is a simple personal finance tracker that brings your credit cards, bank accounts, debts and monthly budget together in one app. See at a glance when each card's statement closes, when the payment is due and how much you have spent this month.

ALL YOUR CREDIT CARDS ON ONE SCREEN
• Add multiple credit cards and set the statement and due date for each
• Instantly see your current balance, this month's statement and available credit
• Mark a payment as paid and your balance updates automatically
• Tell your cards apart with different colors

NEVER MISS A DUE DATE
• Get a reminder before the due date and on the morning of the due date
• Choose how many days in advance you want to be reminded
• See every statement and due date together in the calendar

INCOME AND EXPENSE TRACKING
• Record your salary and other income, and add it straight to an account if you like
• Log your expenses by category
• Compare monthly income and spending, and look back at previous months

ACCOUNTS AND DEBTS
• Keep track of your bank accounts and balances
• Record rent, bills, loans or money you owe someone, sorted by category
• See your net position (accounts minus all debts) on the home screen

PLANNED PAYMENTS
• Plan recurring payments like internet, gym or insurance
• Monthly payments roll over to the next month automatically once paid
• Always know how many days are left

SIMPLE AND CLEAR
• Your cards, monthly summary and upcoming payment together on the home screen
• Choose your own currency
• Add an entry in seconds with the quick add button

PRIVACY AND SECURITY
• FK Wallet does not connect to any bank; it only knows what you enter
• No real card number or CVV is ever requested or stored; cards are identified only by their last 4 digits
• Your data is stored privately in your account and only you can access it
• Your data is not used for advertising and is never shared with third parties

FK Wallet is not a banking app; it does not make payments or transfer money. It is a tracking tool designed to help you keep your budget and payment schedule in order.
